Ghana's budgets, tracked year after year

What Ghana's government planned each year, what the following budget says actually happened, and where the promises fell short. Now covering the budgets, with more added as they are presented. Every figure links back to the page of the Budget Statement it came from.

Closing the loop

Each budget sets targets for its year. The next budget reports what happened. Here the two are put together: each year's targets against the outturn reported in the budgets that follow.

Targets against outturns

Status is judged against the target as originally set. Outturns are often provisional; the basis is shown.
